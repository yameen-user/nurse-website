TERMS & CONDITIONS

Please Read Carefully Before Booking Our Services

Our purpose is to connect patients and their families with suitable home care nursing staff according to their requirements. We aim to maintain professional communication, privacy, transparency and responsible service coordination.

1. Nature of Our Service

Healing Hands Home Care Nursing Services primarily coordinates nursing staff for patients according to their care requirements and staff availability. Our role includes communicating with patients, families and nursing staff regarding duty arrangements, attendance, payment and routine service-related matters.

2. Patient's Medical Condition

  • Patients and their families must provide accurate information about the patient's medical condition, care requirements and any special needs before booking.
  • Nursing staff should be assigned duties appropriate to their qualifications, experience and competence.
  • We do not guarantee recovery or any particular medical outcome.
  • Home nursing services are not a substitute for emergency medical treatment or hospital care when required.
  • In an emergency, the family must contact the treating doctor or emergency services immediately.

3. Nursing Staff Information

  • Nursing staff may have different qualifications, experience and skill levels.
  • Relevant identity, qualification, registration and experience checks will be explained accurately according to the verification actually completed.
  • Patients and families may request available credentials before confirming a nurse.
  • We aim to arrange nursing staff according to the patient's requirements and the information available to us. Specific skills and suitability must be assessed according to the patient's needs.

4. Home Security and Personal Belongings

  • Patients and families are advised to secure cash, jewellery, valuables, important documents, keys and other personal belongings.
  • Families should manage access to their home and personal belongings appropriately.
  • Any suspected theft, misconduct, property damage or other incident should be reported promptly to the appropriate authorities and to our service.
  • Healing Hands does not provide a guarantee against theft, loss, misconduct or other incidents involving individuals at the patient's home. Each incident must be assessed according to the facts and applicable law.

5. Scope and Limitation of Responsibility

  • Our role is primarily to arrange nursing staff and coordinate routine service-related matters, including attendance, duty timings, payment communication and staff availability.
  • Healing Hands does not guarantee the personal conduct, honesty, independent actions or performance of every individual nursing staff member.
  • We do not guarantee patient recovery or freedom from medical complications, deterioration or other unforeseen events.
  • Disputes, alleged misconduct, theft, property loss, personal injury or medical incidents involving patients, families or nursing staff will be assessed according to the circumstances and applicable law.
  • Our responsibility is limited to the services and commitments agreed upon, subject to applicable legal obligations. Nothing in these terms removes any responsibility that cannot legally be excluded.

6. Service Charges and Payment Policy

  • Nursing charges will depend on the patient's condition, level of care required, nursing staff's qualifications and experience, duty hours, shift duration, location and other relevant requirements.
  • All charges, the agency's commission, payment schedule, advance payment (if applicable), overtime and cancellation terms will be communicated before duty begins.
  • Payments from the patient's family must be made to Healing Hands according to the agreed payment schedule.
  • Nursing payments will be settled every 10 days through Healing Hands. The agreed agency commission will be deducted, and the remaining amount will be paid to the concerned nurse.
  • Patients' families must make payments on time. Any delay or payment-related issue must be communicated promptly.
  • Any revision in charges must be discussed and agreed upon in advance.

7. Nursing Staff Conduct

  • Nursing staff are expected to treat patients and family members with dignity, respect, patience and professionalism.
  • Staff must respect patient privacy and maintain confidentiality.
  • Theft, harassment, misuse of belongings, unauthorised sharing of private information and other inappropriate conduct are prohibited.
  • Staff must follow agreed duty schedules, handover arrangements and appropriate patient-safety practices.
  • Patients and families are expected to treat nursing staff respectfully and provide a safe working environment.

8. Privacy and Confidentiality

  • Patients, families and nursing staff must respect confidential medical information, personal details, photographs, videos and home addresses.
  • Private information must not be shared without proper authorisation, except where legally required.
  • Communication should remain professional and related to legitimate care or service requirements.

9. Complaints and Coordination

  • Attendance issues, late arrival, absence, payment concerns and routine service complaints should be reported to Healing Hands promptly.
  • We will communicate with the concerned nursing staff and take reasonable coordination steps within our agreed role.
  • Serious allegations, suspected criminal acts and medical emergencies should also be reported to the appropriate authorities or medical professionals as necessary.
  • Replacement staff may be arranged subject to availability and the agreed service terms. Immediate replacement cannot be guaranteed in every situation.

10. Cancellation and Changes

  • Patients and families should inform Healing Hands as early as possible about cancellations, changes in duty hours or changes in patient-care requirements.
  • Refunds, notice periods, replacement arrangements and cancellation charges will follow the terms agreed upon before booking.
  • Services may be suspended or discontinued for appropriate reasons, including non-payment, serious safety concerns or abusive conduct, with due consideration for patient safety and continuity of care.

11. Acceptance of Terms

By booking our services, the patient or authorised family representative confirms that they have been informed of the applicable service scope, nursing charges, payment schedule and terms and conditions.
